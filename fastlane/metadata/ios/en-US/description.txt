TheVoteApp puts democratic participation in your pocket.

Cast your vote on national policy with cryptographic privacy — your ballot is anonymous, publicly verifiable, and permanently recorded on a tamper-proof blockchain.

KEY FEATURES
• Zero-knowledge proofs — your eligibility is proven without revealing your identity
• Government branding — each country's interface reflects its official design system
• Hardware security key support — use a FIDO2 / YubiKey for the strongest authentication
• Biometric confirmation — Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint before every vote
• Open-source — Apache 2.0 licensed; audit the code at github.com/TheVoteApp

PRIVACY
• Your vote is encrypted on-device before transmission
• No names, no addresses, no personal data stored or transmitted
• The private key never leaves your device's secure enclave
• Fully offline-capable for low-connectivity regions

TheVoteApp is free, non-partisan, and built for every nation on Earth.
